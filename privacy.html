Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026. This policy applies only to Tennis Lab Backups.

Access and use

The utility requests Google Drive drive.file permission and offline access to upload, list, verify, download, and replace its own encrypted backup files in a dedicated private folder. It does not browse unrelated Drive documents.

Data and security

Recovery archives can contain the application's databases, settings, credentials, source code, models, and service/network configuration. Contents and archive filenames are encrypted with rclone crypt before upload. Authorization tokens and encryption keys are stored with restricted permissions on the owner's server and in a separate private recovery copy. They are not published by this website.

Retention and sharing

Only the newest verified successful archive is retained. Older matching backups are permanently deleted after the replacement passes verification. A failed backup preserves the previous successful backup. Unknown files are not removed. The Drive folder remains restricted. Backup data is not sold or shared for advertising, and is not used to train general-purpose AI models.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke access in Google Account third-party connections and delete the dedicated Drive folder. Revocation stops unattended backups. Recovery requires the independently held encryption keys.

Google Drive stores encrypted objects under Google's terms and privacy policy. This utility's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact the operator through the support page. Do not post credentials, tokens, archives, or private application data in public support requests.
